1. Laser gain equation

For laser light of frequency $\omega$ exciting a transition middle frequency $\omega_{21}$ $$ \begin{align} \text{power gained} &= \underbrace{[(N_2A\delta z)B_{21} - (N_1A\delta z)B_{12}]}_{\text{number of atoms in each level}} \cdot \underbrace{\hbar\omega}_{\text{photon energy}} \cdot \underbrace{G(\omega-\omega_{21})}_{\text{linewidth}} \cdot \rho(\omega) \\ &= B_{21}(N_2 - \frac{B_{12}}{B_{21}}N_1)A\delta z \cdot \hbar\omega \cdot G(\omega-\omega_{21}) \cdot \frac{1}{c}\,c\rho(\omega) \\ &= B_{21}\underbrace{(N_2 - \frac{g_2}{g_1}N_1)}_{g_1B_{12} = g_2B_{21}}A\delta z \cdot \hbar\omega \cdot G(\omega-\omega_{21}) \cdot \frac{1}{c}\,\underbrace{I(\omega)}_{I = c\rho} \\ &= (N_2 - \frac{g_2}{g_1}N_1)A\delta z \cdot \underbrace{B_{21} \frac{\hbar\omega}{c} \frac{\omega^2}{\pi^2c^2}}_{A_{21}}\frac{\pi^2c^2}{\omega^2} \cdot G(\omega-\omega_{21}) \cdot I(\omega) \\ &= \underbrace{(N_2 - \frac{g_2}{g_1}N_1)}_{N^*}A\delta z \cdot \underbrace{A_{21}\frac{\pi^2c^2}{\omega^2} \cdot G(\omega-\omega_{21})}_{\sigma(\omega-\omega_{21})} \cdot I(\omega) \\ &= N^* \cdot \sigma(\omega-\omega_{21}) \cdot I(\omega) \, A\delta z \end{align} $$

Now, we need to find the population inversion density $N^*$ in terms of the system's parameters. Start with the rate equations, with pump rates $S_1$ and $S_2$ $$ \begin{align} \dot{N}_2 &= 0 = S_2 - \frac{N^* \sigma(\omega-\omega_{21}) I}{h \omega} - \frac{N_2}{\tau_2} = S_2 - N^* W - \frac{N_2}{\tau_2} \\ \dot{N}_1 &= 0 = S_1 + N^* W - \frac{N_1}{\tau_1} + N_2 A_{21} \end{align} $$ where we have substituted $W = \frac{\sigma(\omega-\omega_{21}) I}{h \omega}$.

Now, isolate $N_1$ and $N_2$ $$ \begin{align} N_2 &= \tau_2 (S_2 - N^* W) \\ N_1 &= \tau_1 \left(S_1 + N^* W + N_2 A_{21} \right) \\ &= \tau_1 \left(S_1 + N^* W + A_{21} \tau_2 (S_2 - N^* W) \right) \\ &= \tau_1 \left(S_1 + (1 - A_{21} \tau_2) N^* W + A_{21} \tau_2 S_2 \right) \end{align} $$

Then, use $N_1$ and $N_2$ to form an equation for the population inversion density $N^*$ $$ \begin{align} n^* &= N_2 - \frac{g_2}{g_1}N_1 \\ &= \tau_2 (S_2 - N^* W) - \frac{g_2}{g_1} \tau_1 \left( S_1 + (1 - A_{21} \tau_2) N^* W + A_{21} \tau_2 S_2 \right) \end{align} $$ Finally isolate and $N^*$ $$ \begin{align} N^* &= \frac{ \tau_2 S_2 \left( 1 - \frac{g_2}{g_1} \tau_1 A_{21} \right) - \frac{g_2}{g_1} \tau_1 S_1}{1 + \left[ \tau_2 + \frac{g_2}{g_1} \tau_1 (1 - A_{21} \tau_2) \right] \frac{\sigma(\omega-\omega_{21}) I}{h \omega}} \end{align} $$
